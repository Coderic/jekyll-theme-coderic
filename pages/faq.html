Answers to the most common questions about Coderic and the theme.

Coderic (Corporation Oriented to the Development of Engineering Resources and Information for Commerce) is an open source software incubator and technology company founded on September 30, 2004. It operates through an ecosystem of specialized portals covering cloud, financial services, development, and more.
Add remote_theme: Coderic/jekyll-theme-coderic to your _config.yml along with the jekyll-remote-theme plugin. See /docs/getting-started/ for the full guide.
Yes. jekyll-theme-coderic is released under the MIT License. It is free for personal and commercial use. Contributions and issues are welcome on GitHub.
Projects incubated at coderic.org that reach commercial stage follow a revenue-sharing model: 80% goes to the developer or team, 20% plus applicable taxes goes to Coderic. The model is documented in full at coderic.org/legal.
Coderic S.A. was incorporated in Venezuela in 2004. Coderic SAS was incorporated in Colombia in 2022. The team is distributed across Latin America; services are available globally.
Community support via GitHub Issues. Enterprise support contracts are available through business.coderic.co. You can also reach us at hello@coderic.org.
